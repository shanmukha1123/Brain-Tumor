Tumors are hard todiagnose owing to their complex structure and variations in
 imaging scans.Early detection and accurate identification are vital for success treat
ment,but contemporary methods are burdened with challenges into segmenting and
 classification of tumors owing to complex forms, restricted labelled datasets, and
 data privacy issues.This research proposes a hybrid approach that combines Con
volutional Neural Networks (CNNs)and Vision Transformers(ViTs) to register 3D
 MRI scans.CNNs learn from clear local features, and ViTs learn from the larger
 spatial, improving performance, as well as trustworthiness. Federated learning guar
antees patient data safety, allowing different institutions to train the model collab
oratively without revealing intensives information.Anisotropic filtering to denoise
 and Generative Adversarial Network(GAN) based data augmentation for extract
ing with data balance sequences are deployed for preprocessing to further augment
 performance.Also,Gradient weighted Class Activation Mapping(Grad-CAM) is in
cluded to point out tumor areas and make the decision of the model more explain
able for medical practice. The developed approach is assessed on the Real time
 image database and out performs current methods on segmentation precision, classi
f
 ication accuracy,and interpretability.The present work shows advantages of mixing
 deep learning model privacy sparing methods in brain tumor diagnosis using med
ical images. Keywords—Tumor,MRIScans,VisionTransformers,Convolutional neu
